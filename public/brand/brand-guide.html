04 / Usage

Make room for the brand.

Decoration supports the content. Preserve the logo, keep the palette cold and leave reading areas calm.

Clear-space illustration; not to scale.

Recommended clear space

Leave at least half the height of the “20 BELOW” letters around the visible logo on every side. Measure from the artwork, including its snow caps.

Keep badges, snowflakes and hard edges outside this space. A faint, soft background glow may sit behind the lockup.

Do

  • Use Snow or Frost drips with rounded ends and varied depths.
  • Keep crystals, snowflakes and brighter details at the edges and corners.
  • Preserve the proportions of the logo, snowflakes and circular seal.
  • Keep textures beneath body text at a final visible opacity of about 12% or less.
  • Use a solid reading area when decoration competes with text.
  • Repeat strips at their natural proportions and tiles at consistent sizes.

Don't

  • Recolor decorative assets outside the six-color palette or add orange heat effects.
  • Rebuild, stretch, crop or recolor the original logo.
  • Place dense frost, sharp icicles or the badge directly over body copy.
  • Add realistic ice, scratchy lines, cloud shapes or heavy shadows.
  • Stretch the repeating tiles to fit a section; repeat them instead.
  • Combine every texture in one section. Choose one main treatment and a small supporting accent.

The two pattern tiles and the grain already contain low alpha. Reduce their layer opacity further when they sit beneath text. Snowflake recoloring above uses local SVG masks; the marks also support currentColor when embedded inline. The seal uses local font fallbacks when viewed as a standalone image.
