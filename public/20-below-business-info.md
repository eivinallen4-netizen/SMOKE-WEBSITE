## 9. Site must-haves for a vape shop

- **21+ age gate** on entry (Nevada's minimum age for tobacco/vape is 21)
- Required tobacco/nicotine warnings on product content
- **No online ordering or shipping of vapes** without checking PACT Act / state rules. Keep it a "visit us" or "call us" site.
- Click-to-call button, directions button, and "Open till Midnight" badge above the fold
- Embed the Google review link as a "Leave us a review" button
